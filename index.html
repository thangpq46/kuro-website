What's inside

Everything a diary should be — and nothing it shouldn't.

Kuro is built around one idea: writing about your day should feel safe, quick and a little bit beautiful.

🔒

PIN & fingerprint lock

Set a 4-digit PIN and unlock with biometrics. The lock is checked on the device — no password ever leaves your phone.

🎨

71 illustrated themes

Moon cities, sleepy cats, paper blooms and starry skies. Every theme restyles fonts, colours and paper in one tap.

🌈

Moods & tags

Pick a mood style you like, tag your entries, and watch the pattern of your weeks appear on its own.

🎙️

Photos & voice notes

Some days are easier to say out loud. Attach pictures and record a note straight into the entry.

📅

Calendar & search

Jump to any date, or find that one evening by a single word you remember writing.

☁️

Backup to your Drive

One tap saves an encrypted-at-rest archive to a private app folder in your Google Drive. Restore it on a new phone.

Themes

Seventy-one moods to write in.

Change the whole diary — paper, palette and typeface — whenever your mood does.

Your data

It stays yours. Always.

  • Entries, photos and recordings live in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • We never upload your diary to our servers — we don't run any.
  • Backups use the drive.appdata scope: a hidden folder only Kuro can see, inside your own Google Drive.
  • Sign-in is optional. Skip Drive and the app still works, forever, offline.
  • Sign out or revoke access any time and the connection is gone.

How it works

Three minutes to your first entry.

Lock it

Choose a PIN and turn on fingerprint unlock. Only you get past the cover.

Write it

Pick a mood, type a few lines, drop in a photo or a voice note. That's a day saved.

Keep it

Turn on Drive backup so a new phone is just a restore away.
