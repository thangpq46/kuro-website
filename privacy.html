Privacy Policy

Applies to Kuro: Private Diary Journal (Android package com.shura.studios.diary) · Last updated September 6, 2026

The short version. Your diary lives on your phone. We do not run servers that store your entries, we cannot read them, and nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you switch on Google Drive backup — in which case the backup goes into your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder only this app can see.

1. Information the app handles

Content you create

Diary entries, titles, tags, moods, photos you attach and voice notes you record are written to the app's private storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us and we have no ability to read them.

Your PIN and biometrics

The PIN you set to lock the diary is stored only on your device. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by Android's BiometricPrompt — the app receives only a yes/no result and never sees your biometric data.

App settings

Preferences such as the selected theme, language, mood style and reminder time are stored locally on the device.

What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account with us, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and we do not sell personal data to anyone.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off by default. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google Account and the app requests a single scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — access to the hidden, application-specific data folder in your Google Drive.

With that scope the app can create, list, download and delete only the files it created itself. It cannot see, open or modify any other file, photo or document in your Drive, and it cannot list the rest of your Drive contents.

A backup is an archive of your entries and their attachments, packed on the device and uploaded straight to that hidden folder. The archive never passes through any server operated by us. Google's handling of the data stored in your Drive is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

When you sign in, the app receives basic Google Account identifiers (such as your account email) purely to show you which account is connected and to make authenticated Drive requests. You can sign out inside the app at any time, and you can revoke the app's access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. Limited Use disclosure

Kuro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained through Google Drive is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you requested; it is not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

4. Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), coarse location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. This processing is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice from the app's settings. Purchasing Premium removes ads from the app.

Your diary content is never shared with advertising partners.

5. Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google — we never receive or store your card details. The app only learns whether an entitlement is active on your device.

6. Permissions the app requests

  • Microphone — only while you are recording a voice note for an entry. Recordings stay on the device.
  • Photos / media — only for images you explicitly pick to attach to an entry.
  • Notifications — to deliver the daily writing reminder you set.
  • Exact alarms & boot completed — so the reminder fires at the time you chose, and survives a restart.
  • Internet & network state — for Drive backup, purchases and ads.

You can deny or revoke any of these in Android settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

7. Retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. Backups you created in Google Drive are not removed by uninstalling: delete them from inside the app before uninstalling, or remove the app's hidden app-data folder from your Google Drive settings.

8. Security

Entries are held in the app's private storage, which other apps on a non-rooted device cannot read, and access to the app is gated by your PIN or biometrics. Traffic to Google Drive uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please also keep your device locked and your Google Account protected.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Your rights

Because your diary is stored on your own device and in your own Google Drive, you already have direct access to it: you can view, edit, export, back up and delete everything from within the app. If you are in a region with data-protection laws such as the GDPR or CCPA and you have a request we can help with, contact us using the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be announced in the app.

12. Contact
